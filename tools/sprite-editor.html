How to use

  • Click or drag to paint. Right-click or hold Shift erases.
  • Hold Alt to pick up the colour under the cursor.
  • Press a palette letter (k, w, c…) to select it, . for transparent.
  • Mirror copies the left half onto the right — how the gear and ball were made.
  • Outline wraps every filled pixel in k, which is what gives these sprites their read.
  • Paste the block into the SPRITES table in js/deck.js, then point a project at it with sprite: yourname in _data/projects.yml.
